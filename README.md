# 🚗 Cardekho Used Car Market Analysis Using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive SQL case study based on the Cardekho Used Car dataset. The objective is to solve **35 real-world business questions** using SQL and extract meaningful business insights from the data.

The project covers data exploration, pricing analysis, vehicle analysis, business insights, and advanced SQL concepts commonly used in Data Analyst roles.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore the Cardekho used car dataset.
- Analyze pricing patterns across brands and models.
- Study the impact of vehicle age, engine size, mileage, and transmission on selling price.
- Identify high-value vehicles using price and power analysis.
- Calculate market share of different brands.
- Apply advanced SQL techniques to solve business problems.
- Generate business recommendations based on data.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Premium brands contribute the highest total sales value.
- Automatic transmission vehicles have a higher average selling price than manual vehicles.
- Newer vehicles generally retain higher resale value.
- Higher engine capacity and maximum power are associated with higher selling prices.
- Vehicles with lower kilometers driven generally have better resale prices.
- Market share analysis identifies the leading brands in the used car market.
- Value-score analysis helps identify cars offering higher power at relatively lower prices.

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

1. Focus inventory on high-revenue brands.
2. Increase availability of high-demand fuel types.
3. Promote premium automatic vehicles.
4. Improve pricing strategy using value-score analysis.
5. Optimize inventory based on brand and model performance.
